I. CORE RULES OF GREEN ZONES
​1.1 GZ - Killing/Damage in GZ
​Rule: Green Zone (GZ) ke andar kisi ko marna ya damage dena strictly prohibited hai, chahe RP justification ya circumstances kuch bhi hon.
​Note: GZ me main facility aur uske aaspas ka area (parking lots, curbs, aur adjacent spaces) shaamil hain.
​Clarification: Law enforcement officers (police) firing ka jawab de sakte hain agar unpar formation ya official duties ke dauran attack hua ho. Fire start karna strictly prohibited hai.
​Punishment: DEMORGAN — 60 minutes + WARN
​1.2 GZ - Criminal Actions in GZ
​Rule: GZ ke andar maujood players ke khilaf koi bhi criminal action mana hai, jaise:
​Kidnappings
​Robberies
​Ropes, bags, ya doosre RP tools ka istemal
​Punishment: WARN
​Repeated Violation: Character ban — up to 3 days
​1.3 GZ - Escaping RP via GZ
​Rule: RP situation se bachne ya bhaagne ke liye Green Zone ka istemal karna mana hai.
​Punishment: WARN
​1.4 GZ - Forcing Players Out of GZ
​Rule: RP tools (jaise ropes) ka use karke kisi player ko GZ se bahar nikalna mana hai.
​Punishment: WARN
​1.5 GZ - Entering GZ During Shootout
​Rule: Chal rahi shootout/fite ke dauran GZ me enter karna mana hai.
​Exception: Agar entry galti se hui ho, toh proof ke liye video recording deni hogi.
​Important: Agar aap GZ ke bahar hain aur kisi aise player ke khilaf action lete hain jo GZ ke andar hai, toh ise GZ rule violation hi mana jayega. Rules todne ki zimmedari aapki hi rahegi, chahe aapki khud ki location kuch bhi ho.
​Punishment: DEMORGAN — 30 minutes
